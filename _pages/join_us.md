##### post-doc opportunities
**bullab** is happy to host post-docs or fellowship holders who bring their own funding. Czechia offers excellent opportunities for international scientists, including Maria Curie Fellowships CZ, where projects scoring more than 70% are funded by the Czech government. 

##### PhD opportunities
**bullab** is happy to host PhD students who bring their own funding (e.g. Fulbright Scholarships). The Faculty of Environmental Sciences provides partial scholarships to PhD students, as long as they secure part of the funding themselves or via their supervisor. Currently, **bullab** does not have funds for PhD positions, but funding may become available in the future (currently 2026), depending on the success of our grant applications.

We strongly encourage Mexican nationals who wish to pursue a PhD at the CZU by supporting their application for a SECIHTI grant (Secretaría de Ciencia, Humanidades, Tecnología e Innovación; formerly known as CONACyT). This grant funds health insurance and monthly stipend of €1,090 (or €1,362 for students with family). The CZU will be able to supplement SECIHTI’s monthly stipend up to (€ X). While the next call for SECIHTI grant is expected in March 2026, it is necessary to collaboratively prepare a project proposal (on ecology, macroevolution, or ornithology in general) beforehand, so please contact us as early as possible. Other SECIHTI funds are also available to do a research stay at our lab or a sandwich PhD.

##### master's & undergraduate options
Our goal is to prepare you for a scientific career. Thus, we aim to support projects that lead to publication. Contact us if you are interested in pursuing your Master's or Bachelor's thesis with us.
